LOOSE CARD WORKFLOW

Capture one card, then move to the next.

  1. Lay the signed card flat in good light.
  2. Tap Take photo / choose scan.
  3. If the QR is readable, the spot fills automatically.
  4. Confirm the spot and save. The next spot loads automatically.

SHEET WORKFLOW

One photo captures four signatures.

  1. Keep all four corners of the printed sheet visible.
  2. Photograph it straight-on or upload a scan.
  3. Ink & Flash splits the page into four signing areas.
  4. Save all four, then verify them in Review & Pair.
Sheet 1 = spots 001–004, sheet 2 = 005–008, and so on.
