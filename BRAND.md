# Ink & Flash — Brand Guide

## Core identity
**Brand:** Ink & Flash  
**Tagline:** Sign. Selfie. Send.  
**Descriptor:** Modern guest books, selfie stations and shared event albums.  
**Brand promise:** Keep the ink. Catch the moment.  
**Short pitch:** Real handwriting and real faces, paired into one keepsake.

## Brand idea
**Ink** is the physical memory: the real signature, card, page, note and handwriting.  
**Flash** is the living memory: selfies, candid photos, the photo station and the event album.  
Ink & Flash exists to keep both together.

## Product family
- **Ink Sheets** — print-at-home signature boxes and lines
- **Ink Cards** — loose QR signature cards in multiple sizes
- **Ink Stickers** — QR labels for an existing guest book
- **Ink Book** — ready-made QR signature guest book
- **Flash Share** — one event-wide QR for guest photo uploads
- **Flash Station** — tablet selfie station with one preselected event look
- **Flash Wall** — optional moderated live photo wall
- **Keepsake Book** — final paired signature + photo coffee-table book

## Voice
Short, confident and playful. Explain technology only when necessary. The guest should hear verbs, not features.

Preferred language:
- Sign. Selfie. Send.
- Keep the ink. Catch the moment.
- Real ink. Real faces.
- Share the smiles.
- Your people, from every angle.
- No app. No account. Just scan.

Avoid:
- SaaS
- workflow
- platform
- software subscription
- technical QR/database language on consumer pages
